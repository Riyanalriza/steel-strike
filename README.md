Steel Strike is an offline tactical first-person shooter that runs entirely in a desktop browser from static files — no server, no network requests.

Two squads of five fight over two objectives on the Ironworks map: the attacking squad plants the device, the defending squad holds the sites and defuses it. Win rounds to earn credits, buy better weapons between rounds, and take the match.

- **Plant / defuse:** attackers carry the device to Site A or Site B and hold the plant; defenders defuse before the timer runs out.
- **Buy economy:** round wins and eliminations earn credits for rifles, SMGs, snipers, shotguns, pistols and armor.
- **Bot squad:** play offline against a full bot team with flanking, retreating and site-holding behavior.
- **Complete game:** start screen, buy phase, rounds, half-time side swap, match win/lose screens and restart — with HUD, kill feed, hitmarkers and procedural sound.

Play it: serve `game/` with any static server and open `game/index.html` in desktop Chrome. Entry for [best-game-01](https://make.fun/bounty/best-game-01) on make.fun.
